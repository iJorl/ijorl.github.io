## Claude's suggestions

A curated paper map of flow matching, from foundations through 2026. To be edited / pruned / expanded as the post takes shape.

### 1. Foundations / precursors

<Paper
  title="Neural ODEs"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07366"
  authors="Chen et al."
  venue="NeurIPS 2018"
>
Parameterizes the hidden-state derivative with a neural net; the seed of continuous normalizing flows.
</Paper>

<Paper
  title="FFJORD"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.01367"
  authors="Grathwohl et al."
  venue="ICLR 2019"
>
Scales CNFs via Hutchinson's trace estimator; trains by maximum likelihood with simulation-in-the-loop.
</Paper>

### 2. The 2022–2023 breakthrough

<Paper
  title="Flow Matching for Generative Modeling"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02747"
  authors="Lipman, Chen, Ben-Hamu, Nickel, Le (Meta)"
  venue="ICLR 2023"
>
Simulation-free regression on closed-form conditional velocity fields; recovers diffusion as a special case.
</Paper>

<Paper
  title="Rectified Flow"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03003"
  authors="Liu, Gong, Liu"
  venue="ICLR 2023 spotlight"
>
Straight-line interpolants + reflow → one-step sampling.
</Paper>

<Paper
  title="Stochastic Interpolants"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08797"
  authors="Albergo, Boffi, Vanden-Eijnden"
  venue="2023"
>
Unifies flow and diffusion via a tunable noise level. [Precursor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15571): Albergo & Vanden-Eijnden, 2022.
</Paper>

<Paper
  title="Conditional Flow Matching / OT-CFM"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00482"
  authors="Tong et al."
  venue="TMLR 2024"
>
FM with arbitrary source distributions; minibatch-OT couplings straighten flows. (`torchcfm`.)
</Paper>

<Paper
  title="Multisample Flow Matching"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14772"
  authors="Pooladian, Ben-Hamu, Domingo-Enrich, Amos, Lipman, Chen"
  venue="ICML 2023"
>
Non-trivial source–target couplings via minibatch OT; straighter flows, fewer NFEs. Sister paper to OT-CFM.
</Paper>

<Paper
  title="On Kinetic Optimal Probability Paths"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06626"
  authors="Shaul, Chen, Nickel, Le, Lipman"
  venue="ICML 2023"
>
Kinetic-energy view of FM paths; characterizes kinetic-optimal interpolants.
</Paper>

<Paper
  title="Action Matching"
  url="https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06662"
  authors="Neklyudov et al."
  venue="ICML 2023"
>
Learns continuous dynamics from temporal marginals — snapshots, not trajectories.
</Paper>

The canonical 100+ page reference + PyTorch library.
</Paper>
